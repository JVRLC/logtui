# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include using
welcoming language, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive
feedback, and showing empathy. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment,
trolling, insulting comments, publishing private information, or other conduct
that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable
behavior. Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported using
the private contact method documented by the project owner. All reports will be
reviewed promptly, fairly, and confidentially.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
